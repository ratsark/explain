# L1 — Principles

## Principles

## P1 — Collision avoidance is an independent last line of defence
status: adopted
serves: G2
assumes: A1

It must work when the ground system that normally separates traffic has
failed, so it may depend on nothing outside the aircraft except other
aircraft's transponders.
