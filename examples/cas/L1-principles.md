# L1 — Principles

## Principles

## P1 — Time to closest approach matters more than distance
status: adopted
serves: G1

The protected volume around the aircraft is shaped by time-to-go, not range:
a slow closure at short range is less urgent than a fast one further out.

## P2 — Advisories change vertical rate only
status: adopted
serves: G2
assumes: A2

Climb or descend, never turn: vertical manoeuvres are quicker to execute and
easier to coordinate between two equipped aircraft. This rests on the threat
not manoeuvring against the advisory [[A2]].
